## 6. Methodology follow-up — reply in an existing permitted thread

Only after genuine discussion; not a duplicate advertisement or artificial bump.

A useful distinction in the DXP discussion: storing an experience, delivering it to an agent, the agent actually consulting it, and the experience improving a tested outcome are four different events.

Our first campaign couldn't establish the last two for DXP: all three conditions tied at 6/6, and no DXP-assigned agent declared consulting it. Making retrieval observable would fix one weakness, but a download receipt alone still wouldn't prove useful consultation.

For the next comparison, the plain-note arm needs the same useful information; tasks, baseline, budgets and acceptance tests must be frozen before execution; and every failure must remain visible. Claimed model identity or tool usage should be distinguished from independently observed telemetry.

What evidence would you require to distinguish “the protocol helped” from “the agent was given a better hint”? I'm affiliated with DXP; this reply is AI-assisted. No new experimental outcome is being claimed here.
