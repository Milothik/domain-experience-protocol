## Question and decision

Does sharing prior experience improve new task completion, and does the DXP layer add value beyond the **same information** in a strong flat note or ordinary Git? A functioning website, accepted JSON, a delivery receipt, community interest and an author's explanation are not evidence of task benefit.

At the deadline, publish one decision about the tested implementation: adopt within the demonstrated scope; simplify to a flat/Git system if its extra machinery is not justified; or stop this implementation if critical governance fails. Publish uncertainty explicitly. Insufficient sample/access means **not validated and do not adopt**, not mathematical refutation of every possible experience protocol. A nonsignificant difference is not equivalence. No automatic extension after the deadline.

## Outcomes and gates, fixed before confirmatory results

Primary: independent acceptance success on unchanged evaluators plus common regression checks. Denominator includes timeouts and abandoned allocated runs. Failure before allocation is infrastructure; failure after a valid allocation is recorded with its cause, never silently rerolled.

Secondary: end-to-end observed wall time, tool calls, actual tokens/cost if available, and capture/retrieval/verification maintenance overhead. Unknown cost remains null. Do not claim savings from self-reported calls or successful runs only. Also measure harmful transfer, stale advice acceptance, false quarantine, traceability and retained contradictions. Do not replace task utility with number of records.

Unit of inference: task cluster, pairing conditions within each model and averaging repeated runs; show raw counts and per-model results. Compute task-cluster bootstrap 95% intervals (10,000 draws, seed 16102026). Compare DXP against both flat and Git, with Holm-adjusted paired randomization tests at 0.05. Report all outcomes, not the most favorable representation or model.

**Accuracy gate:** DXP must improve observed success by at least 10 percentage points over both strong baselines, with adjusted evidence above zero, and a positive point estimate within both model configurations. Any narrower success is labeled local/exploratory. Small samples may fail this gate even with a promising point estimate.

**Efficiency alternative:** a separately reported gate: success noninferiority lower 95% bound above -5 percentage points against both baselines, and total cost/time ratio upper 95% bound at most 0.80, with reliable end-to-end telemetry including preparation. Missing telemetry cannot pass this gate.

**Governance gate:** zero observed severe quarantine/private-scope bypasses in the frozen acceptance set; report denominator, false rejections and counterexamples. Passing a finite suite does not prove universal security. A corrected bug does not erase its initial failure.

Adopt only if a utility gate and governance gate pass, reproducibility is demonstrated and scope is explicit. Otherwise do not adopt DXP as a performance improvement. If a strong baseline meets operational requirements at equal/better quality and observed total cost, prefer simplifying. Evidence of robust local harm refutes the corresponding local hypothesis. Low precision or absent model access leaves empirical utility unresolved; it does not force a positive or universal negative claim.
